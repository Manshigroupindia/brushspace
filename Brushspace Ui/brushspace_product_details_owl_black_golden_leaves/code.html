Each Owl Black vase is wheel-thrown individually in our studio using mineral-rich clay harvested from rural Khurja. The dramatic silhouette evokes an abstract owl guardian, an age-old symbol of quiet wisdom.

Following high-temperature firing, our craft masters gently treat the surface with a hand-burnished matte oxidation coat. Real 24k-sheen gold foil leafing is meticulously adhered alongside delicate glass bead mosaics across three separate drying sessions.

Overall Height: 32 cm (12.6 inches)

Base Footprint: 11 cm flat stable base
Net Weight: 1.82 kg (Dense stoneware)
Firing Atmosphere: Reduction kiln, 1220°C

Designed primarily for dry floral arrangements, pampas grass, sculptural twigs, or solitary pedestal display. Do not fill with untreated water as this is an unglazed decorative oxidation finish.

To clean: Dust gently with a clean feather duster or wipe using a soft dry microfiber cloth. Avoid commercial liquid chemicals or abrasive sponges to preserve the gold leaf leafing integrity.

Every piece is encased in custom-moulded shockproof EPS casing and packed inside a reinforced 7-ply corrugated exhibition box.
