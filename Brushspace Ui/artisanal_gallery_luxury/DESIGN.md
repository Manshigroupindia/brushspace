## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the serene curation of an upscale modern gallery combined with the tactile warmth of handcrafted decorative arts. It communicates thoughtful luxury, quiet confidence, and bespoke craftsmanship through structured architectural minimalism, generous negative space, and disciplined material styling.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Crafted for design connoisseurs, interior curators, and design-minded homeowners seeking collectible décor objects. The interface evokes stillness, refinement, and tactile longing—making every vase, tray, and mosaic piece feel like an exhibited masterwork rather than a catalog commodity.

### Design Movement: Warm Architectural Minimalism
The system avoids transient visual trends like glossy skeuomorphism or loud brutalism. Instead, it pairs classical editorial typography with razor-sharp structural framing, whisper-thin divider lines, and warm plaster-like tonal foundations. It relies on architectural proportion, deliberate rhythm, and museum-grade negative space to let the objects lead the narrative.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earthen pigments against dark architectural framing to create a museum-like backdrop for sculptural home objects.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#A67C38` - Antique Bronze):** Used intentionally for key focus accents, active interactive highlights, subtle metadata rules, and refined CTA fills.
- **Secondary (`#1F1B18` - Deep Espresso):** Serves as high-contrast structural text, frame lines, and primary button backgrounds for commanding authority.
- **Tertiary (`#C36B4E` - Muted Terracotta):** Reserved for curated spotlights, seasonal badges, narrative highlights, and warm secondary calls to attention.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5` - Warm Ivory/Plaster):** The atmospheric canvas color, providing warmth and depth absent in pure digital white (`#FFFFFF`).

### Application Rules
Maintain a strict light-mode ambiance where light clay tones (`#F5F0EB`, `#E8DFD5`) form quiet contextual cards and subtle container tiers. Avoid pure black and vivid digital primaries; deep espresso anchors the visual hierarchy with warmth and timeless weight.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects museum curation: humanistic, literary serif display headings joined with crisp, utilitarian sans-serif support.

### Typographic Roles
- **EB Garamond (Headings & Editorial Leads):** Delivers grace, historical presence, and artisanal authenticity. Used for collection names, editorial stories, object titles, and showcase quotes. Headings should preserve relaxed normal tracking, avoiding heavy bold weights to maintain light optical delicacy.
- **Inter (Interface, Product Specs, Forms):** Provides modern, highly legible functional clarity. It handles navigation menus, prices, product descriptions, cart drawers, and dashboard tabular data.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Micro-labels, collection categories, and curated taglines use `label-md` with `0.12em` letter-spacing in uppercase Inter to introduce refined architectural precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a 12-column architectural grid on desktop, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Generous Canvas Margins:** Sections are framed with extensive vertical and horizontal breathing space (`space-xl` and above) to simulate the quiet spacing of physical exhibition halls.
- **Grid Asymmetry & Rhythms:** Product showcases use staggered column spans (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired with 5 columns of editorial context) rather than rigid, dense e-commerce grids.
- **Reflow & Responsive Adaptations:** On screens under 768px, horizontal multi-column galleries transition into single-column vertical flows or horizontal touch-snapping carousels with fixed `margin-mobile` bleed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on structural planes and subtle tonal separation rather than overt drop shadows.

### Elevation Principles
- **Atmospheric Tonal Layers:** Depth is achieved by placing neutral cards (`#F5F0EB` or `#E8DFD5`) over the warm ivory canvas (`#FBF9F5`). Containers sit flat within the layout structure.
- **Hairline Framing:** Micro-dividers and structural edges use low-contrast borders (`rgba(31, 27, 24, 0.08)`) to establish order without visual clutter.
- **Ambient Floor Shadows:** For floating elements (drawers, modals, dropdown menus), use a warm, diffuse ambient shadow tinted with Deep Espresso:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(31, 27, 24, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 27, 24, 0.03);`
- **Object Lift:** On interactive product cards, elevation is signaled via a slight scale transition (`scale(1.01)`) and border warm-up rather than vertical displacement shadows.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural shape language (Level 1 roundedness).

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.25rem):** Form inputs, buttons, chips, badges, and notification toasts use clean, subtle 4px corner rounding. This preserves geometric precision while avoiding harsh needle-sharp corners.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem):** Imagery frames, exhibition cards, and modal dialogs adopt an 8px radius to gently temper large layout blocks.
- **Drawers & Floating Sheets (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem):** Slide-out carts and bottom sheets apply 12px curvature exclusively on exposed canvas corners.
- **Strict Avoidance:** Full pill shapes and heavily rounded circles are prohibited for core interactive elements to protect the brand's architectural aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Deep Espresso (`#1F1B18`) with Warm Ivory text (`#FBF9F5`), 0.25rem radius, and Inter Medium. Hover shifts to Antique Bronze (`#A67C38`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Framed with a 1px border (`#1F1B18`), transparent background, and Deep Espresso text. Hover introduces a sand tint (`#F5F0EB`).
- **Tertiary (Text/Editorial):** Borderless with an underline offset by 4px, featuring an inline chevron indicator for collection discovery links.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp 1px border (`rgba(31, 27, 24, 0.15)`), tinted Ivory background (`#FFFFFF` or `#F5F0EB`), with 0.25rem radius. Focus state activates an Antique Bronze hairline outline (`#A67C38`) without aggressive outer rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Scaled down to 16px with precise 1px stroke framing. Selected states fill with Deep Espresso or Antique Bronze with white interior glyphs.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curatorial Badges:** Compact uppercase tracking (`label-sm`), background tinted with sand (`#E8DFD5`), and text rendered in Deep Espresso.
- **Status/Material Tags:** Bordered with hairline outlines and zero heavy fills, signaling materials such as "Hand-blown Glass" or "Mirror Mosaic."

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Cards:** Generous padding around imagery, soft sand-toned canvas backing (`#F5F0EB`), 0.5rem radius, and subtle typography hierarchy (Serif object name, clean sans spec line, Antique Bronze price).
- **Curated Collection Banners:** Asymmetrical split layouts matching editorial imagery directly against narrative text panels with minimal architectural grid separations.
